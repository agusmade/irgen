# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent and give constructive feedback.
- No harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior.

## Scope
This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and community channels.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to: krisnaparta@gmail.com

The maintainers will review and take appropriate action, including warnings or bans.
